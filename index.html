Welcome

About me

Hello! I am Julianna Harden, a PhD student in Plant Biology at Cornell University (Specht Lab), where I am interested in characterizing the endophytic fungal diversity associated with the culturally and agriculturally significant tropical tree, Theobroma cacao, and its wild relatives. Additionally, I am interested in investigating the functional roles of the fungal communities in relation to host plant health (e.g., plant growth and susceptibility to fungal pathogens). My research seeks to address the association between fungal diversity and Theobroma cacao’s susceptibility to pathogenic fungi to promote its success in agroforestry systems in the tropics. In addition to the intellectual insights, my research incorporates a strong social element, focusing on how cacao-producing communities recognize and manage fungal diseases.
